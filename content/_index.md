I am a Ph.D. candidate in the [Department of Economics](https://economics.cornell.edu/) at [Cornell University](https://www.cornell.edu/) and a fellow at the [Brooks School of Public Policy](https://publicpolicy.cornell.edu/). My advisor is [Amanda Agan](https://sites.google.com/site/amandayagan/).

I work on crime, labor, and political economy. Most of my research asks how institutions in the criminal justice system shape the behavior of the people inside them, from the police officers deciding whom to arrest to the employers deciding whom to hire.
